Session details

    Record the session 0:00:00

    Put the phone in the middle of the table, plugged in, with the app open. Set Auto-Lock to Never for the session (Settings → Display & Brightness): recording pauses if the screen locks. The audio is saved in small pieces as you go, so any length works.

    Transcribe with Whisper

    Runs on this phone, free and private. The first time, it downloads the Whisper model you picked in Settings.

    Upload a recording

    Transcribe a file with Whisper on this phone. MP3 and WAV work at any length. M4A (Voice Memos) works best under about an hour; for longer ones, record here instead or export as MP3.

    Live captions instead

    Uses the iPhone's built-in speech recognition to write the transcript as you play. Quicker but less accurate than Whisper, and it can't run while you're recording, so pick one per session.

    Transcript
    Edit or paste a transcript

    Already have a transcript (from Voice Memos, Buzz, or anything else)? Paste it here or choose a .txt, .vtt or .srt file. Timestamps and caption numbers are cleaned up automatically.

    Settings

    Whisper model

    Bigger models are more accurate but download more and take longer. Small is the best balance for a busy table on a recent iPhone. Models download once and stay on this phone.

    How notes work

    Notes are written by Claude through copy and paste, so nobody needs an API key or a paid plan. On the Notes tab, tap Copy for Claude, paste into the Claude app (a free account works), then paste Claude's reply back. The app files the recap, NPCs, characters, lore and arc story for you.

    Backup

    Everything lives on this phone. Back up now and then, or before switching phones. Recordings aren't included in backups; transcribe them first.
